About the Tech Hub Club

Empowering students through technology, innovation and leadership.

Club History

The Tech Hub Club of the Department of Computer Science, Lady Keane College, was established in 2016 to promote innovation, collaboration and technical excellence among students.

The club regularly organizes workshops, coding competitions, awareness programmes, seminars and technical events to help students improve their programming, analytical and leadership skills.

🎯 Mission

To create an environment where students learn, innovate, collaborate and grow through technology.

🚀 Vision

To become a leading student technology club that inspires creativity, leadership and excellence.
