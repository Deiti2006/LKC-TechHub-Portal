About the Tech Hub Club

The Tech Hub Club of the Department of Computer Science, Lady Keane College, Shillong, is a student-led club established to encourage learning, innovation, collaboration, and technical excellence. The club provides students with opportunities to enhance their knowledge, participate in technical activities, and develop leadership and teamwork skills.

Club History

Established in 2016, the Tech Hub Club has been actively promoting computing knowledge and student engagement through various academic and co-curricular activities. The club was created to support students of the Department of Computer Science by fostering interest in computing, mentorship, social interaction, and professional development. It continues to inspire students by organizing events, competitions, workshops, and awareness programmes.
